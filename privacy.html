Privacy note

Website enquiries and product feedback

The form builds a draft in your browser. This website does not submit the fields to a server, store them in a database, or send your name, email, website or notes to Google Analytics.

Choosing “Send enquiry on WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with the details you entered in its link. WhatsApp receives that draft when you open the link, even before you send a message. You can edit it there. Other WhatsApp buttons start a chat with a suggested message about the package, demo or product.

Choosing “Prepare an email draft” shows a local preview. Opening Gmail or your email app passes the recipient, subject and message to that service. You can also copy the draft. If you send a message, I use the information you share to reply to your enquiry or feedback.

Demo websites

The three fictional demos preview sample requests in your browser. The restaurant basket lasts only while the page is open. Please use made-up details when trying the forms.

Hosting, fonts and analytics

GitHub Pages serves the published portfolio. The homepage and demos request fonts from Google. These providers receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects.

The published homepage uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and interactions and may use analytics cookies. Enquiry events count WhatsApp button clicks by location, openings of fictional demos by name, and email drafts prepared. These event labels contain no form answers. A click measures interest and does not confirm that a message was sent. Local previews do not load the analytics tag.

Questions about your information

Use the contact details on the homepage for privacy questions or to request deletion of an enquiry you sent.
