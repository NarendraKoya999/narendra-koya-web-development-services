Privacy note

Website enquiries and product feedback

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email app. You decide whether to send it. If you send an email, I receive your address and the details you share, and use them to reply about your project or feedback. You can also use the phone link to call me.

The WhatsApp links open a chat with a suggested message. You can edit it before sending. The website does not copy your enquiry form answers into WhatsApp. I use messages you send to reply about your project.

Demo websites

The three fictional demos preview sample requests in your browser. The restaurant basket lasts only while the page is open. Please use made-up details when trying the forms.

Hosting, fonts and analytics

GitHub Pages serves the published portfolio. The homepage and demos request fonts from Google. These providers receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects.

The published portfolio homepage loads Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies. The enquiry form prepares the message locally in your browser. Local previews do not load the analytics tag.

Questions about your information

Email narendra.koya.in@gmail.com if you have a privacy question or would like me to delete an enquiry you sent.
