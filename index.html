GOOD QUESTIONS

Wondering
about something?

Here are a few things people often ask before starting a website project.

Ask me anything ↗
How much does a website cost? ＋

It depends on the number of pages, features, content and integrations. After a short conversation, you'll receive a clear quote before deciding whether to proceed.

How long will it take? ＋

Timing depends on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback are available. I'll agree on a realistic timeline with you before work starts.

Do I need to understand technology? ＋

Not at all. I'll explain choices in everyday language and guide you through anything you need to provide or approve.

Will my website work on mobile phones? ＋

Mobile-friendly layouts are part of the plan. Key pages and actions should be checked on different screen sizes before launch.

Who owns my website and domain? ＋

Ownership, domain registration, hosting access and handover arrangements should be agreed in writing. I recommend that you retain control of your domain and important accounts.

Can you update or maintain my website later? ＋

Ongoing updates and maintenance can be discussed as a separate arrangement. The scope, response times and costs should be clear before support begins.
